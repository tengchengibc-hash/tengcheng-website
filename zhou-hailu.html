Zhou Hailu · MoFort Packaging

Zhou Hailu is an industrial product R&D and manufacturing professional focusing on Paper IBC containers, heavy-duty paper packaging, structural design, and manufacturing process innovation.

Through MoFort Packaging, he works on paper-based bulk packaging solutions for suitable liquid media, food ingredients, chemicals, resin pellets, metal parts, and other heavy-duty cargo.
