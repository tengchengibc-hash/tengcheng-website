Why MoFort

Engineered around transport reality

Seamless continuous wrap

The sleeve is continuously wound on a mandrel with no vertical overlap; corner shear stress is transferred to normal compression across the wall.

ISTA 3E tested

The 1000L packaging unit completed the ISTA 3E transport sequence at a third-party laboratory. Report No. 2026-Y009-211, 14 May 2026.

Paper-based end-of-life

The outer body enters the standard corrugated recycling stream; the liner is sorted and processed separately.

Factory & Capacity

Production base and capacity

MoFort is operated by Qingdao Tengcheng Packaging Co., Ltd., based in Jimo, Qingdao. Figures below are current approximate values; final scheduling and lead time are confirmed per order.

About 5,000 ㎡ plant

Continuous-wrap forming, liner and pallet pre-assembly, and finished-goods staging for sample validation and batch delivery.

Around 50,000 sets / year

Combined capacity for Paper IBC and heavy-duty boxes; scheduling varies by model, ply build and accessories.

Outgoing moisture control

Outgoing board moisture is held at 8.5%–9.0%; below 7.0% triggers humidification review and above 10.5% is a hold-line rejection.

Team of 11–50

R&D, forming, QC and delivery teams coordinate on sample validation, batch scheduling and after-sales support.
