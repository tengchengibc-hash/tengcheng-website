Application Directions

Typical packaging directions for food ingredients, non-hazardous liquid chemicals and heavy solid cargo. Suitability, rated load and stacking are confirmed against the actual product, test reports and operating conditions. This solution is not presented as suitable for hazardous goods, strong acids/alkalis, or high-temperature and high-pressure duties.

Non-hazardous liquid bulk

Edible oils, syrups and condiment bases, paired with food-contact liner options and bottom discharge.

Non-hazardous liquids

Industrial liquid packaging

Heavy solids · export

Heavy bulk paper packaging

Hardware castings, auto parts and high-density plastic pellets with cushion liners and a fumigation-free structure.

High-viscosity

High-viscosity food discharge

Tomato paste, syrups and similar viscous products with a bottom discharge port and high-barrier liner to reduce residue.

Pallet replacement

Cost

From drum management to box management

Paper-based packaging avoids drum cleaning, drum return and deposit management; actual cost comparison is calculated per operating condition.

Compliance

Transport test and export quarantine

The 1000L packaging unit completed the ISTA 3E transport sequence at a third-party laboratory (Report No. 2026-Y009-211, 14 May 2026); the all-paper construction is fumigation-free.

End-of-life

Paper-based sorting

The outer body enters the standard corrugated recycling stream; the liner is sorted and processed separately. Flat folding reduces storage and return-space use.
