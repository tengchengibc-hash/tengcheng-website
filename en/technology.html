Technology & Certification

Seamless continuous wrap, gradient wet-press corners and ISTA 3E verified transport performance.

Seamless structure

No seams. No shear points.

The sleeve is continuously wound from 6–8 plies of liner board and A-flute medium. With no vertical overlap, compression load spreads evenly and bulge resistance improves by up to 40% versus seamed boxes.

Key structure

Cross-section
Octagonal: 4 faces + 4 bevelled corners
Fold-flat
Twin crease lines + relief cavity, zero spring-back
Assembly
No nails, no metal frame, no fumigation
Patented corner

Foldable corner structure that doesn't crack

Our utility patent CN 223764942 U combines outer angle faces, arc faces and buffer faces. When folded, an internal triangular relief cavity absorbs the doubled wall thickness — zero corner cracks, zero spring-back, even on 30–40 mm boards.
